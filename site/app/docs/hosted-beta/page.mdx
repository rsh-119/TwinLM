# Hosted beta

Try TwinLM without installing anything. The hosted beta is a free, shared TwinLM: sign up, and you get your own
team, an API key and a monthly budget on free AI models. When the beta is open, the **Try it free** button on the
[home page](/) takes you to sign-up.

## What you get

- **Your own team**, as its admin. Nobody else sees your keys or usage.
- **API keys** for your apps: the same OpenAI-compatible API as a self-hosted TwinLM. Change `base_url` and
  `api_key`, nothing else ([Integrate your apps](/docs/integrate/)).
- **The console**: the playground, usage per day and per model, keys, and your team's budget.
- **Free models**: `auto` and `fast` try Groq first and fall back to Gemini; `smart` tries Gemini first. Both use the
  providers' free tiers.
- **A monthly budget** (about $1 of list-price usage). Requests that would go over it are refused until the
  next month.

## Limits

| Limit | Why |
| --- | --- |
| About 10 requests and 20,000 tokens per minute per key | The free models' limits are shared by every beta user |
| Replies up to 1,024 tokens | Keeps every request's worst-case cost inside the budget |
| No conversations or memories | Nothing you send is stored as text on the beta |
| May sleep when idle: the first request after a pause can take about a minute | Free hosting |
| No uptime promise; data may be reset; sign-ups are capped | It's a beta on free tiers |
| No password reset by email yet | Ask on [GitHub](https://github.com/rsh-119/TwinLM/issues) and we reset it |

## Your data

- Your prompts pass through the beta server to **Groq** or **Google (Gemini)**, the providers that answer them.
  Free tiers come with the providers' own terms, which may allow them to use free-tier data to improve their
  services. Don't send anything confidential.
- TwinLM itself records **request metadata** (time, model, tokens, cost, latency, status), not prompts or replies.
  Conversations are switched off on the beta.
- Your email, name and a hashed password are stored for sign-in.

## For real traffic, self-host

The beta is for trying TwinLM. For production, run your own: your prompts then never leave your infrastructure,
you use your own provider keys and models, and there are no shared limits.
